Cash & Carry

In this game, you'll run a tiny retail business for four quarters. Margins are thin and you pay for inventory up front. Your goal is to post the highest net income without ever running out of cash.

Three rules of the ledger

  1. Revenue is recorded when you make the sale, even if the customer pays you later.
  2. Cost of goods sold is recorded when a unit sells — not when you buy it. Unsold units sit on the balance sheet as inventory.
  3. Cash is tracked separately. Inventory is paid for the moment you order it, customers may pay late, and bills come due. Cash moves only when money actually changes hands.
